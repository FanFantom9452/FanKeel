WHICH COMMIT THEY RAN AGAINST — NOT RECORDED, AND THAT MATTERS

The runner does not write the tree's HEAD into its output, and these ran against
an uncommitted working tree during another task's build. So the sha cannot be
stated. What can be stated is which commits bracket them:

  15daa59  2026-09-10 20:43  fix: the graders count Task, a when key must be a
                             profile key, four pages catch up
  3771e7e  2026-09-10 20:51  fix(docs): the separator regex survives
                             JSON.stringify, and four sentences say what is so

The four runs at 20:10-20:11 therefore predate 15daa59. eval-subagent-no-entry-task.json
at 20:43 sits at it — it is the re-run of that one case after the rename, which
is why it carries `-task` in its name.

THE VERDICTS, AS THE FILES CARRY THEM

  eval-one-call-not-agent      no-agent-dispatch pass  reads-the-file FAIL
  eval-pipe-not-agent          no-agent-dispatch pass  pipe-or-grep FAIL
  eval-stage-skip-said         no-stage-off-route pass says-which-stages-skipped FAIL
  eval-subagent-no-entry       at-most-one-start pass  dispatches-a-reader FAIL  no-edit-or-write pass
  eval-subagent-no-entry-task  at-most-one-start pass  dispatches-a-reader FAIL  no-edit-or-write pass

Every positive grader failed and every negative grader passed. The judgement
reads that pattern as a property of the graders rather than of the model: three
of them match a tool named `Task`, and this harness names its dispatch tool
`Agent`, so `min: 1` could never pass and the two `max: 0` could never redden.
The `-task` re-run at 20:43 is the evidence for that reading — the rename landed
and `dispatches-a-reader` failed exactly as before.

`reads-the-file` was a separate defect, a regex that could not match a Windows
backslash, and 3771e7e fixed it at 20:51.

`says-which-stages-skipped` is the one the judgement calls a real behaviour
signal. n=1 cannot separate a prompt failure from a model one; the same tree
scored 2/3, 3/3 and 1/3 on evals/route-typo across three runs on 09-08 and 09-09.
Five runs on one commit is what would settle it, and that spend has not been
authorised.
