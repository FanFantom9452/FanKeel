## one elision, named

eval-route-typo.json and eval-stage-skip-said.json had their per-run `raw` key removed
before committing: it held the whole stream-json transcript, about 520K across the two
files, against a convention where the largest previous evidence directory is 141K. Each
run now carries a `rawElided` string saying how many lines went and the exact command
that produced the run. What is kept is everything the report cites -- `graders` with the
regex or tool predicate behind each verdict, `cost` per run, `toolCalls`, and
`lastMessage`, which is the exact text the regex graders matched against, so the failing
grader's own input is still here. A re-run makes different transcripts, so those lines
are gone rather than reproducible; that is the cost of the elision and it is why it is
written down here rather than left for a reader to notice.
